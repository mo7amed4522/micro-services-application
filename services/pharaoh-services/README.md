# Pharaoh Services

## Overview

The Pharaoh Services is a specialized gRPC microservice that manages comprehensive data about ancient Egyptian pharaohs. This service provides detailed historical, genealogical, religious, and archaeological information about Egyptian rulers, making it ideal for educational applications, historical research platforms, and gamification systems.

## Features

### Historical Data Management
- **Comprehensive Pharaoh Profiles**: Complete biographical and historical data
- **Dynasty Organization**: Pharaohs organized by Egyptian dynasties and periods
- **Chronological Information**: Reign dates, succession, and historical timeline
- **Family Lineage**: Detailed genealogical relationships and family trees

### Educational Content
- **Rich Multimedia**: Images, audio narrations, and documentary videos
- **Archaeological Data**: Tomb discoveries, burial sites, and artifacts
- **Religious Context**: Divine associations, temple affiliations, and religious reforms
- **Cultural Achievements**: Building projects, military campaigns, and political accomplishments

### Gaming & Engagement Features
- **Rarity System**: Common, Rare, Epic, and Legendary classifications
- **Character Traits**: Leadership, military, diplomacy, wisdom, and charisma stats
- **User Ratings**: Community-driven popularity scoring
- **Unlock Mechanics**: Game progression and achievement systems

### Search & Discovery
- **Advanced Search**: Multi-field search across names, epithets, and achievements
- **Filtering Options**: By dynasty, period, rarity, and other criteria
- **Pagination Support**: Efficient data retrieval for large datasets
- **Sorting Capabilities**: Multiple sorting options for organized browsing

## Technical Architecture

### Core Technologies
- **Language**: Go 1.23
- **Framework**: gRPC with Protocol Buffers
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Configuration**: Environment-based with .env support
